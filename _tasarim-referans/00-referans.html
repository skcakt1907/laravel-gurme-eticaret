About the Aegean

Along Turkey's Aegean shore, warm currents and clean waters have made grey-mullet roe a prized delicacy for centuries. It is here that AEGEA RESERVE begins — with wild fish, sea salt, and the patience of sun and wind.

Every piece is traceable, wild, and cured the traditional way, so the flavor of the Aegean reaches your table exactly as it should.
